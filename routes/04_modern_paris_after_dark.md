### Investigation 1 — Belphégor at the Louvre: KEEP

Historical importance is television history; narrative value is exceptionally strong. INA documents the four-part serial beginning in March 1965 and contemporary viewers organizing their evenings around its Louvre phantom. The Louvre also programmed the series decades later. The apparition is **FICTION / CULTURAL ASSOCIATION**, not evidence of an actual haunting. [INA retrospective](https://www.ina.fr/ina-eclaire-actu/1965-belphegor-un-feuilleton-a-succes-avant-l-heure-du-replay), [Louvre programme](https://mini-site.louvre.fr/trimestriel/2015/Agenda_du_Louvre_Dec15_Fev16/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf)

**Site / why selected:** use Cour Carrée near the fountain, facing Pavillon de l’Horloge. This is an interpretive viewpoint, not an authenticated scene location. The enclosed façades make the museum an architectural container whose interior cannot be seen. Cour Napoléon is a fallback; its pyramid belongs to a later Louvre.

**Explainer:** Look at the windows. During the day, this building promises knowledge: labels, galleries, familiar masterpieces. Once access ends, its contents become an act of imagination. In 1965, television supplied a hidden occupant: Belphégor. Millions followed the fictional apparition through successive Saturday broadcasts. The ghost was invented; the public fascination was real. A building can acquire another life through the stories people watch together. From this courtyard, you do not need to see the phantom. The question is what your own imagination places behind those closed doors.

**Notice:** controlled entrances, unreadable windows, enclosing walls; compare the old palace with its modern entrance. **Access:** Cour Carrée 07:30–19:45, Cour Napoléon 07:30–21:00; public river pavement remains a weaker fallback. Museum admission is unnecessary. [Current Louvre hours](https://www.louvre.fr/en/visit/hours-admission?tab=musee-du-louvre)

**Overlap:** Cinema Paris strong (screen stories rewriting actual places); Paris of Illusions medium (constructed identity). Bibliographic dates and precise interior filming claims are unnecessary and left unresolved.

### Investigation 2 — Éléonore beneath Rue du Pont-Neuf: KEEP

**DOCUMENTED FACT:** a roughly 80 cm Nile crocodile was recovered by firefighters on 7 March 1984 beneath Rue du Pont-Neuf. The official record identifies the street, not the bridge or an exact hatch. Her arrival remains unexplained; abandonment by an exotic-pet owner is a plausible account, not a solved case. A city exhibition later used her to discuss the boundary between reality and myth. [Paris Council resolution](https://a06-v7.apps.paris.fr/a06/jsp/site/plugins/solr/modules/ods/DoDownload.jsp?id_document=161183&items_per_page=20&query=Agent+sp%C3%A9cialis%C3%A9+de+la+Pr%C3%A9fecture+de+Police&sort_name=&sort_order=&terms=Agent+sp%C3%A9cialis%C3%A9+de+la+Pr%C3%A9fecture+de+Police), [City exhibition announcement](https://cdn.paris.fr/presse/2020/03/23/48e208bcf7842cd51514abb42f10d945.pdf)

**Site / why selected:** stand on Rue du Pont-Neuf where a pavement permits a brief pause. The ordinary street is the point: an implausible creature occupied everyday maintenance infrastructure. No original rescue marker or exact chamber was verified. Any drainage cover is illustrative only.

**Explainer:** Read the street sign first: Rue du Pont-Neuf. Beneath this street in 1984, firefighters recovered a young crocodile. The rescue is documented; how she arrived is not. The familiar tale of an unwanted pet may explain it, but explanation is not proof. For people above ground this was commerce and traffic. For workers below it was infrastructure, until an unfamiliar animal appeared. One crocodile does not prove a breeding colony. It does show how a true event can give later underground fantasies a foothold. At the Louvre we met a fictional creature with real cultural consequences; here the apparently fictional creature was real. [City account](https://www.paris.fr/pages/un-crocodile-a-vecu-dans-les-egouts-de-paris-en-1984-19564)

**Notice:** street sign and surface activity; the absence of a monument; drains only as illustrations. **Access:** free public street, three-minute stop, no sewer entry. **Overlap:** Paris Below strong; Engineered City medium; Paris of Illusions medium. Her exact later death date and any commemorative installation remain unverified and are omitted.

### Investigation 3 — Catacombs exterior: BORDERLINE, removed from standalone geometry

Film France CNC verifies the 2014 film *As Above, So Below* used the Paris Catacombs. This is a genuine production association with a **FICTIONAL** supernatural labyrinth. The public entrance itself was not established as a recognizable filming location. Outside 1 avenue du Colonel Henri Rol-Tanguy, the walker sees a visitor threshold, but neither ossuary nor galleries. The narrative value is stronger than the outdoor site value. [Film France CNC](https://www.filmfrance.net/en/once-upon-a-time/as-above-so-below/)

**Reserve explainer:** From this square, the underground has an ordinary address and an entrance. A 2014 horror production filmed in the actual Catacombs and transformed them into supernatural terror. The location connection is real; the nightmare is scripted. Standing outside exposes the distance between a managed visitor site and the inaccessible world a film asks us to imagine.

**Notice / access:** modest entrance against the large square; visitor arrangements, not an alleged secret entrance. The site reopened 8 April 2026; current Tuesday–Sunday hours 09:45–20:30, last entry 19:30, Monday closed. An interior visit would improve physical payoff but needs its own booking and budget decision. Do not mistake the stale English closure notice for current French operational information. [Official reopening](https://www.catacombes.paris.fr/actualites), [official practical information](https://www.catacombes.paris.fr/visiter/informations-pratiques)

**Architect decision:** remove the long southward extension; keep this explainer only if another route naturally reaches Denfert or includes entry. Overlap Paris Below very strong; Cinema Paris strong. No older death legends added.

### Investigation 4 — Trocadéro clandestine cinema: SKIP for this route

Contemporary reporting corroborates an equipped screening space discovered in the Chaillot quarry area in 2004. Reports attribute it to La Mexicaine de Perforation; published depth estimates differ. The existence of the reported discovery is stronger evidence than all the theatrical detail that accumulated around it. No exact public surface marker or current visitable cinema was established. [Le Monde contemporary report](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/2004/09/11/la-mexicaine-de-perforation-fait-du-cinema-sous-le-trocadero_378737_1819218.html)

AFP describes a projector, seating and bar, plus accounts of recorded barking discouraging intruders and a message left after the equipment was removed. Those vivid particulars are **REPORTED ACCOUNTS**, sometimes attributed to other newspapers or participants, rather than archaeological evidence available to the walker. A subsequent report raised a spoof interpretation; treat promotional embellishment and discovery circumstances cautiously. [AFP report](https://mg.co.za/news/south-africa/2004-09-07-police-find-underground-cinema-in-paris/), [Independent caution](https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/europe/what-lies-beneath-5351623.html)

The Chaillot esplanade could illustrate conspicuous tourist Paris above concealed culture, but it would be an interpretive viewpoint rather than a verified vertical pin. The detour does not earn a western redesign. Preserve as an investigation lead for Paris Below (very strong overlap), Cinema Paris (medium) and Paris of Illusions (medium). No public entrance instructions or claimed current access supplied.

## Compact reserve module — Imagined Ghost, Real Monster

This module preserves two investigated stories if the controller wants a light evening option. It is not a substitute full route and does not occupy a protected route slot automatically.

- **Thesis:** modern Paris creates creatures through screens and sometimes discovers them in its infrastructure.
- **Vibe / guide:** playful unease and a factual reversal; Noctámbulo.
- **Start:** Cour Carrée, around 48.8604, 2.3380; finish Rue du Pont-Neuf at the Quai du Louvre end, around 48.8598, 2.3424. Coordinates are approximate interpretive standing points, not event pins.
- **Distance:** roughly 0.8–1.3 km including approach through the Louvre courts; a provisional estimate, unmeasured by pedestrian routing.
- **Duration:** 25–45 minutes including stories and courtyard time.
- **Order / core:** Belphégor → Éléonore; both core. Walk out toward the river façade and Rue du Pont-Neuf. The transition switches from an invented monster believed collectively to a real animal that sounds invented.
