# Portfolio Controller Review — Paris 2026

Review date: 2 October 2026. Scope: portfolio arbitration across the manifest and Routes 01–20. This is not external Claude acceptance and does not replace measured-route, live-access, or spoken-delivery QA.

## Executive decision

Keep a **working portfolio of 16 route identities**, of which **13 are protected or strong candidates** and **3 remain conditional**. Do not try to consume all 28 theoretical half-day slots. Friend time, bed/rest days, weather, and ordinary food/laundry/logistics should be expected to remove several slots. A realistic planning envelope is:

- **13–16 route blocks actually walked**, leaving 12–15 of the 28 theoretical slots uncommitted or available for friend/rest/recovery and rescheduling;
- at most **one long 4.5–6 hour route** in a day;
- two compact blocks may share a day only after lodging approaches, museum circulation, and return transit keep the total within the 20k/30k/40k bands;
- retain at least **six completely unassigned half-day slots** until friend plans and fatigue patterns are known.

The portfolio does not need another theme to meet morning supply. Its main weaknesses are conditional physical sites in Spy Paris, calendar concentration around operating days, and too many attractive paid options competing for a narrow residual budget.

01 Lovers; 02 Old Devils; 03 Spirits and Spectacles; 05 Republic; 06 Revolutionary; 07 Occupied; 08 Art Nouveau; 10 Burlesque; 12 Market; 13 Engineered; 14 Paris Below; 17 Scientific; 19 Illusions.

### Flexible candidates — 3 identities

11 Cosmopolitan while the verified exhibition remains; 15 Paris Reads as weather/refuge and lower-priority variety; 18 Canal as a free outdoor daylight option.

### Conditional commissions — 2 identities within the 16-route working cap

09 Spy pending physical validation; 16 Cinema pending actual programme and merge integration. Promote them only after their gates pass. If one fails, do not invent a replacement merely to preserve a count.

### Modular treatment

20 Colonial Afterlives remains one thematic file but two operational modules. It can replace a failed conditional route, provide a low-load near-NOC block, or consume two separate slots if interest and energy justify it. Route 04 no longer counts independently.

This yields **16 active route identities plus the two-module Colonial reserve**, not 20 obligations. Walking all protected, flexible, both conditionals, and both colonial modules would consume about **18 half-day-ish slots**. The recommended actual target is **13–16 slots**, leaving substantial room inside the theoretical 28.

## Time-slot supply

Using each route’s independent evidence rather than its label:

- **Strong morning supply:** 01, 02, 03, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09 conditional, 12, 13, 14, 17, 18, 19, 20. This is more than half the portfolio; morning supply is not a problem.
- **Strong afternoon supply:** nearly all daylight routes, especially 01, 02, 05–09, 11, 13–20. Route 12 is not an equivalent afternoon route.
- **Genuinely strong evening supply:** 10 Burlesque. Route 02’s exterior variant and the Route 04→16 coda are useful early-evening material. Route 16 can be strong only around an actual programme. There is no harmful evening congestion.
- **Genuinely strong night supply:** none as a full free-standing route. Burlesque can extend into night on a real performance night. This is acceptable: do not manufacture night routes from closed cemeteries, museums, gardens, or unreadable façades.

Scheduling cautions:

- **Wednesday/Saturday morning:** protect for Route 12’s best market form.
- **Tuesday–Friday or Sunday:** required for complete Route 07; Monday loses its opening, Saturday loses its ending.
- **Thursday–Saturday evening:** best window for Route 10 with actual activity.
- **Wednesday–Saturday, non-holiday:** preferred for Route 17’s full main line; holiday dates can cancel the demonstration even when admission is free.
- **Tuesday 3 November 2026 afternoon:** currently the dated Route 16 Pathé candidate; treat it as a fragile event dependency, not a permanent route fact.
- **Sunday 1 November:** free-admission opportunities compete. Rodin, Panthéon, Sainte-Chapelle, and Porte Dorée cannot all be meaningful raids in one day. Choose at most one major free-day anchor plus one geographically/physically compatible compact block.

## Narration revision priorities

Routes 01–05 need revision because their delivery standard predates the stronger 06+ benchmark. Revision effort is not equally valuable:

1. **Route 02 — highest value.** S priority, excellent physical anchors, strong folklore/fact distinctions. Expand the delivered site encounter while keeping the compact four-stop shape.
2. **Route 03 — highest value.** S priority and unusually strong objects. The three cemetery narrations deserve rehearsal-level pacing, respectful cemetery conduct, and a clean distinction among staged apparition, belief, and later folklore.
3. **Route 05 — high value after ownership edit.** Remove Hôtel de Ville as a core, then revise the remaining citizenship arc so it does not feel like three isolated institutional façades. Recalculate geometry before prose polish.
4. **Route 01 — worthwhile.** C priority but a strong human route. Revise the six core narrations, especially the transition from private relationships to public simplification; do not spend revision time on the closed wall or Bir-Hakeim.
5. **Route 04 — no standalone revision.** Edit only the Belphégor→Éléonore module for integration into Route 16. Do not polish rejected ghost-station or Catacombs extensions.

For Routes 06 onward, prioritize operational and spoken QA over wholesale rewriting. Route 09 needs physical-site validation before more prose; Route 16 needs programme confirmation before more prose; Route 20 needs module separation rather than longer narration.

## External QA gates

No route is externally accepted yet. After controller edits, send viable artifacts through genuine external Claude evaluation for:

1. **Actual pedestrian geometry:** legal/current paths, crossings, segment distances, backtracking, hills, cemetery paths, and transit-separated modules.
2. **Geographic coherence:** especially 01, 05 after ownership change, 06’s westward return, 09’s wide arc, 11’s multi-neighbourhood line, 16’s event/coda integration, and 20’s split blocks.
3. **Physical payoff:** current visibility/scaffolding and whether the walker sees enough at each core. Critical for 09, 19, and façade-heavy 08.
4. **Theme and overlap:** verify that Route 05 still works without Hôtel de Ville, Route 09 remains distinct from 07, Route 13 remains distinct from 18, and Route 11 remains distinct from 20.
5. **Explainer quality:** Spanish spoken rhythm, factual density, clear fact/legend/interpretation transitions, connection to visible details, and memorable endings. Routes 01–05 require the dedicated revision pass first.
6. **Operational truth:** refresh all 2026 hours, dated events, exhibition dates, ticket prices, reservations, holiday exceptions, cemetery/garden gates, construction, security access, and transit disruptions shortly before scheduling and again before execution.
7. **Museum discipline:** verify each paid/free raid still contains 6–10 currently visible objects/groups and that loans/room closures do not erase the payoff.
8. **Load and degradation:** measure route plus NOC approach/return and any paired block against 20k/30k/40k day bands; verify a short, honest degradation path and real refuge/transit exits.
9. **Final artifacts:** only after acceptance produce or validate coordinates, ordered segments, JSON/YAML, photographs, and the low-token route website.

External Claude evaluation remains pending for every route; this review makes no claim that it has occurred.
