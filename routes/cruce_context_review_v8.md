## Finding

The v7 response correctly learned the **narrative method** from the Granada
anchors, but made a generic portfolio mistake: it confused recovering layered
waypoints with proving that every recovered waypoint belongs in one continuous
walk.

The 7.104 km number hides the problem rather than solving it:

- 3.995 km is a transfer from Nerval to the Père-Lachaise gate with no mystery
  chapter;
- Cour des Miracles creates a 1.590 km southward hinge and then a long eastward
  reversal;
- Capucines repeats Robertson before the visitor reaches Robertson's much
  stronger surviving monument;
- the cemetery must operate in daylight, while the Capucines script says
  “Esta noche”; the proposed product is explicitly *not* a night walk;
- exact Capucines ground, Cour provenance and cemetery paths were still unaudited
  when the proposal was promoted;
- changing a protected, researched three-stop highlight into a 4.5–6 hour
  cross-city route disregards the trip rule that a compact experience may be a
  highlight without being inflated into a full walking day.

This is precisely the kind of route-count pressure the handoff prohibits.

## Controlling correction

**The Dead Become Public remains a protected compact signature experience, not
the sixteenth full-walk architecture.** Its canonical operating route is:

1. Robertson monument, division 8;
2. Allan Kardec monument, division 44;
3. Victor Noir monument, division 92;
4. exit toward Gambetta.

It is approximately 2.5–4 km and 2–3 hours. Its importance comes from its arc
and density, not its mileage:

> manufactured apparition → claimed communication → real murdered body
> transformed by living ritual

The existing researched scripts are retained. They should be revised against
the two calibration anchors: direct the eye first, establish the human fact,
change register explicitly, let uncertainty enlarge rather than cancel the
story, and make Victor Noir converge rather than summarize.

## What survives from v7

The work is not discarded:

- the two Granada examples remain the canonical calibration anchors;
- the Capucines, Cour des Miracles and Nerval drafts remain a **separate central
  dark-city module/workbench**, subject to their listed evidence audits;
- Nerval remains the strongest optional prelude, but should be joined by transit
  or scheduled separately rather than counted as four kilometres of thematic
  walking;
- Capucines can be used when the exact standing point is resolved, especially
  for an illusions/science context, but must not duplicate the cemetery lecture;
- Cour des Miracles remains conditional because the hostile-source history is
  the subject, not a licence to repeat a criminal kingdom as social fact;
- the proposed Robertson, Kardec and Victor Noir edits are useful drafting
  material, but must be integrated into the full existing scripts rather than
  published as disconnected replacement fragments.

## Route and trip consequence

The portfolio returns to **15 full-walk architectures plus protected modules
and signature experiences**. That is not a demotion of the paranormal work.
For itinerary priority, the compact signature outranks any provisional green
route whose narration does not pass commissioning.

Do not make a traveller spend nearly four kilometres crossing Paris merely so
the highlight clears a spreadsheet threshold. Schedule the cemetery experience
in daylight, leave room for attention and emotion, and pair it only with rest,
food, friend time or a genuinely optional central module—not with filler.
