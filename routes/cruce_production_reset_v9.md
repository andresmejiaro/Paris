## Acceptance
A route earns its place through its theme, physical experience, story and practical duration. Seven kilometres is a classification reference, not an acceptance threshold. Short experiences can be primary outings. Do not add an address, scenic lap or chapterless transfer to pass a distance threshold. Do not reject a compelling route because it is 6.7 km.
Fifteen is the inherited architecture pool, not a completion quota or a ban on a distinct user-requested experience. The gastronomic commission is explicitly authorized. Route length and total day load are separate quantities; include approach, food and transit walking when scheduling. Repeated 30k-step days have cumulative cost.

## Narration
Use the two Granada examples as a method, not a repeated template. Keep people, atmosphere, sensory framing, consequences and payoff. Distinguish fact, testimony, legend and interpretation at the point where the register changes. Avoid repeating research disclaimers throughout the spoken text. Keep source caveats in evidence notes except where uncertainty is the story.
A gate, exact ground, altered building or meaningful absence may be an excellent dark waypoint. A plaque or surviving crime room is not required.
