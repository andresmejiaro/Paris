## 1 — Spy Paris: physical-anchor resolution

### Thematic screen

The question was physical legibility, not distance. Two findings are materially different:

1. **Passy passes.** The stop should be fixed at the cut corner of **1 rue Singer / rue Raynouard**, approximately **48.85539, 2.27966**, rather than the former broad search pin at 48.8571, 2.2800. The Société historique d’Auteuil et de Passy inventories a memorial at 1 rue Singer; a specialist memorial registry supplies a photograph, exterior-wall placement, and inscription text. The old residence does not survive, but the tall 1910 inscription explicitly marks the vanished dépendance and Franklin’s residence. This is a physically legible “trusted access in a lost headquarters” threshold, not a claim that the current school is Franklin’s house. [Historical-society plaque inventory](https://www.histoire-auteuil-passy.org/archives/grands-personnages-du-xvie-arrondissement/benjamin-franklin); [US War Memorials marker record and photograph](https://www.uswarmemorials.org/html/monument_details.php?MemID=1357&SiteID=499); [Paris Musées record of the former Franklin pavilion at 60 rue Raynouard](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/musee-carnavalet/oeuvres/maison-de-franklin-rue-raynouard-a-passy-aout-1909-actuellement-maison-des).
2. **Mata Hari remains visually failed/conditional.** The historic arrest address remains exact and the Defence biography remains the appropriate authority for the case, but current official records establish major works, not an uncovered historic hotel. A City Council record describes scaffolding across **103–111 avenue des Champs-Élysées** and the giant trunk-like advertising wrap; a later City technical dossier still identifies the entire 103–111 block as a Louis Vuitton worksite. Neither certifies what will be exposed on the trip date. [Defence Ministry biography](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/chemins-memoire/histoire-memoires/personnalites/personnalites-lettre-e-lettre-m/mata-hari); [City Council record of the construction wrap](https://cdn.paris.fr/paris/2024/01/26/bovp-debats-nov-2023-0Nkr.pdf); [City 2025 block dossier](https://cdn.paris.fr/paris/2025/04/30/103-avenue-des-champs-elysees-8eme-t13ab4-ns-micro-4g-bouygues-OeA4.pdf).

The Passy result solves the route’s minimum physical-anchor problem because Hôtel Beauharnais already supplies the second exact, legible operation. Mata Hari may remain as an unusually honest final contrast between an archival event and a present surface that withholds the historical setting. It cannot carry the route, cannot be described as a surviving hotel façade, and cannot be called “currently wrapped” without a near-date recheck.

## 2 — Cosmopolitan North and East: CENTQUATRE insertion

### Why CENTQUATRE passes the theme screen

**CENTQUATRE-PARIS, public entrance 5 rue Curial**, is not included merely because international artists use it. The City describes it as a municipal establishment of diffusion, residency, and production for artists from around the world, with substantial fabrication/production space. In 2026 its own programme makes exchange operationally exact: **Villa Formose Immersive** is a bilateral France–Taiwan prototype residency; the France residency runs mid-November to mid-December 2026. The official Villa Formose roster identifies Taiwanese immersive creator Jamie Hsieh at CENTQUATRE in November. The chapter can therefore explain how exchange is produced—workspace, equipment, mentoring, prototype development and distribution networks—rather than infer culture from passers-by. [City production/residency description](https://www.paris.fr/pages/lieux-d-accompagnement-de-residence-et-de-repetition-aux-metiers-d-art-3542/); [CENTQUATRE Villa Formose programme](https://www.104.fr/appels-a-artistes/villa-formose-immersive); [official Villa Formose resident roster](https://www.villaformose.tw/).

The public experience also works without pretending a residency studio is open. CENTQUATRE explicitly lists its halls as free/open spaces for artistic practice, Tuesday–Friday 12:00–19:00 and weekends 11:00–19:00, while its main public entrance is 5 rue Curial. The walker may observe actual shared artistic labour in those halls if present; the named resident, a rehearsal, or a prototype must **not** be promised. [Official practical information](https://www.104.fr/infos-pratiques).

This insertion also improves the line conceptually: ICI shows contemporary artists transforming inherited stories; CENTQUATRE supplies another institutional model in which cross-border work is prototyped and produced; Bouffes supplies performance collaboration; Brady supplies commercial translation; Belleville remains a contested lived/public-space finale. It is cultural-production evidence, not a fifth nationality label.

## 3 — Northwestern Belt: from fortification edge to constructed nature

### Candidate comparison and thematic choice

- **Square Alexandre-et-René-Parodi / Maillot threshold — KEEP.** The City’s impact study makes the historical sequence unusually exact: the Thiers wall marked the edge; its demolition produced boulevards/green belt; the 1970s périphérique and roundabout severed the square from the Bois; the current project extends Parodi and a green way back across the périphérique. This is the missing metropolitan-edge chapter, visible in paths, planting, road decks and the Bois threshold. [City impact-study summary](https://cdn.paris.fr/paris/2020/08/27/87051a6a43e19aa05a26231331997a66.pdf); [City project/status page](https://www.paris.fr/pages/la-porte-maillot-se-transforme-en-place-19599); [official square page](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/square-alexandre-et-rene-parodi-2752).
- **Jardin d’Acclimatation gate / little railway — useful connective, not a core or paid stop.** The institution documents the Porte-Maillot railway’s successive horse, steam and electric traction and a current 1.6 km ticketed ride. That is relevant transport history, but entering or riding would change the route into a paid attraction and its human-exhibition history requires a much larger critical chapter. Pass the public threshold only; do not count interior distance. [Jardin history](https://www.jardindacclimatation.fr/160-ans-histoire); [current railway page](https://www.jardindacclimatation.fr/attractions/le-petit-train).
- **Mare Saint-James — KEEP as observation, not as a loop.** Its value is the visible artificial landscape between the reconnected threshold and the surviving gate: the City attributes the remade Bois to Alphand/Barillet-Deschamps and its park architecture to Davioud. Do not claim that every current bank or rock is original without object-level research. [City Bois history](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/bois-de-boulogne-2779).
- **Porte de Madrid — KEEP as endpoint.** The gate gives an exact, exterior payoff rather than an arbitrary turn-around. City archives date construction of a Porte de Madrid pavilion to 1857; Carnavalet preserves Marville’s 1858–62 photograph of the neo-Gothic guard house; the City locates the gate at boulevard Richard-Wallace / boulevard du Commandant-Charcot. The vanished François-I château is background, not the primary route claim. [Archives de Paris inventory](https://archives.paris.fr/_depot_ad75/_depot_arko/ead/INV1900.pdf); [Carnavalet/Paris Musées guard-house record](https://parismuseescollections.paris.fr/de/node/173763); [City Porte de Madrid location/history](https://ws-paris-v4.paris.fr/pages/de-porte-en-porte-paris-se-raconte-16658).
- **Fondation exterior — REJECT for this route.** It is visually strong but would hand the finish to a branded cultural building, not metropolitan edge/constructed nature, and encourages a ticketed detour. It is not needed for distance.

Porte de Madrid is an exterior gate-area finish; do not promise an open pavilion interior. A daylight route sheet should name ordinary paved fallback roads between Parodi, the Jardin threshold, Mare Saint-James and Madrid.

## Controller synthesis recommendation

Batch E earns **three full-walk slots**, but not at equal confidence:

1. **Cosmopolitan** is the cleanest rescue: the added stop independently passes the theme and the same tested line reaches 7.356 km.
2. **Northwestern Belt** passes at 7.690 km and finally has the missing metropolitan-edge payoff; its risk is operational path quality, not thematic padding.
3. **Spy** should pass architecture because Passy is now locatable and legible, Beauharnais remains exact, and geometry already passes. Preserve an explicit operational condition: recheck the Passy inscription and Champs hoarding close to departure. If the Passy inscription is unexpectedly covered or inaccessible, demote/rebuild rather than allowing Mata Hari’s construction wrap to become the sole endpoint evidence.
