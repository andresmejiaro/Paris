## 1. Elevated East / Coulée Verte — managed woodland payoff

### Thematic screen

**Pass.** The inherited route explains railway infrastructure converted into a
linear garden and then Alphand's manufactured lake/wood. A final crossing of
the managed Bois to **La Ferme de Paris, 24 route de la Tourelle** changes the
scale again: the City describes this as a five-hectare municipal farm and a
demonstration/resource site for agroecology, eco-grazing, permaculture and
sustainable food production. This is a real contemporary management chapter,
not simply another attractive patch of woodland. The City's Bois history also
states that Alphand created the curving promenades, four lakes and connecting
river system after Napoleon III transferred the wood to Paris for public
recreation. Sources: [Ferme place and current hours](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/la-ferme-de-paris-6597),
[renovated Ferme and new public entrance](https://mairie12.paris.fr/pages/la-ferme-de-paris-renovee-un-lieu-ressource-au-service-de-l-agriculture-urbaine-et-de-l-alimentation-durable-32433),
[Bois history](https://mairie12.paris.fr/pages/le-bois-de-vincennes-10288).

The Arboretum was rejected as the payoff even though it would fit the subject:
the official listing says it is closed **28 August–1 December 2026** after the
27 August storm. It contributes zero. [Official Arboretum notice](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/arboretum-de-paris-1796).

### Access/closure gate

The [official Coulée verte listing](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/coulee-verte-rene-dumont-1772)
must be refreshed immediately before walking: corridor sections and seasonal
hours are real constraints, and lift outages do not authorize drawing a route
through a locked access. The Ferme's October 2026 listing shows limited public
opening (Wednesday afternoon and weekends in the displayed period), advises
arrival 30 minutes before closing, and warns of weather closures. On a closed
day the farm exterior is still an exact endpoint but loses much of the payoff;
schedule this route for an open afternoon or keep it conditional. Woodland
paths also require a post-storm check. The southeast finish is close to bus 77;
do not count the exit journey as thematic mileage.

## 2. Southern Margins — buried river, light housing, planted relief and campus edge

### Thematic screen

**Pass.** PC13 is unnecessary. The replacement line makes the buried Bièvre's
physical consequences visible: the Moulin-des-Prés trace and markers lead from
the Butte into low ground; Cité Florale's light houses were built in 1928 on a
former marsh/flood area where heavier buildings were unsuitable; Montsouris is
an engineered nineteenth-century landscape laid over former quarries; and the
CIUP is a planned southern housing/campus margin. These are four different
responses to difficult ground and metropolitan expansion, not generic pretty
streets. Sources: [City history of Cité Florale](https://www.paris.fr/pages/nouveaux-quartiers-du-cote-de-la-place-de-rungis-2845/),
[City Montsouris history and current hours](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/parc-montsouris-1810),
[Apur Bièvre historical/topographic study](https://www.apur.org/fr/climat-environnement/nature/bievre),
[City account of the buried Bièvre](https://www.paris.fr/pages/les-secrets-enfouis-de-la-bievre-riviere-parisienne-16900).

### Closure/access gate and title

The [official PC13 listing](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/petite-ceinture-du-13e-pc-13-18089)
continues to control: while its open-ended post-fire closure notice remains,
PC13 contributes **zero**, even if a map router offers it. The surface candidate
does not approach or depend on it. Refresh Montsouris gates (the official page
currently displays 07:00–19:30 for 1–24 October 2026), walk only public Cité
Florale streets and respect residents, and keep the CIUP to confirmed public
park/perimeter ways. Recommended working title: **Southern Margins — Buried
Bièvre, Housing and Planted Ground**. Do not retain “Petite Ceinture.”

## 3. Western Green Machinery — two retired-rail conversions

### Thematic screen

**Pass.** A PC14 opening supplies the missing eastern retired-rail chapter and
creates a useful comparison: its 0.75 km cutting was deliberately kept relatively
wild, followed by a surface handoff to the former Vaugirard slaughterhouse/rail
landscape, then the now continuous two-kilometre PC15. The City's current PC15
listing says that since **August 2026** a secured 300 m tunnel joins the former
sections, gives five exact entrances, and explicitly connects Georges-Brassens
to Balard. It also explains that the railway served the Vaugirard slaughterhouses
and Citroën works before those industrial sites became the two parks. This is
precisely retired rail + industrial land + park design, not distance repair.
Sources: [current PC15 listing, entrances, history and hours](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/petite-ceinture-du-15e-pc-15-16841),
[City ecological-promenade lengths, including PC14](https://paris-v4.paris.fr/pages/la-petite-ceinture-et-ses-promenades-ecologiques-7855),
[Georges-Brassens slaughterhouse remains](https://www.paris.fr/pages/paris-un-musee-a-ciel-ouvert-au-travail-17501).

### Access/closure gate

Use only the official PC14 accesses at the Coulmiers/Général-Leclerc and Didot
ends; never continue on closed railway beyond them. Refresh both PC14 and PC15
notices on the day. The current PC15 page lists **47 rue de Dantzig**, 101
Olivier-de-Serres, 397ter–399 Vaugirard, opposite 82 Desnouettes and 84 Leblanc
at Balard, and displays 09:00–18:30 weekday / 09:30–18:30 weekend hours for
1–24 October 2026. Parks can close for weather and seasonal hours. The island
finish requires the lawful Grenelle stair/bridge approach; the short return to
Pont de Grenelle is an exit, not added thematic mileage. Bir-Hakeim is omitted,
and no Water-route narration is reused.

## Controller recommendation

Advance all three to operational verification. Southern is the least fragile:
it passes entirely on surface public streets/parks and should be renamed now.
Western has become materially stronger because the City now certifies the
August 2026 PC15 connection that earlier geometry could only treat as proposed.
Elevated passes strongly on distance and subject, but should be scheduled around
the Ferme's limited opening; the storm-closed Arboretum must not be offered as
an alternative.
