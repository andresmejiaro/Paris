Controller correction, 3 October 2026. The paranormal route **did survive**,
but the v6 synthesis described it too quietly as a distance-failing module. That

researched route remains one of the project’s most distinctive experiences.

The companion `layered_dark_waypoint_reassessment_v6.md` also reopens several

can support the mystery without pretending to prove a haunting.

Its canonical identity is:

## The Dead Become Public — Spectacle, Spiritism and a Murdered Body

**Status: PROTECTED SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE.**

**Core:** Père-Lachaise, lawful public paths, approximately 2.5–4 km and 2–3
hours depending navigation, looking, quiet pauses and cemetery conditions.

**Waypoints:**

1. Robertson monument, division 8;
2. Allan Kardec monument, division 44;
3. Victor Noir monument, division 92;
4. exit toward Gambetta.

This three-stop form is the canonical protected route. It was never
rejected or replaced by Cinema; the restored central chapters now give it the
full-length architecture that the earlier object gate had wrongly removed.

## Why this is still the highlight

The three stops make an unusually clean progression:

### 1. Robertson — fear is manufactured

Robertson’s tomb shows the audience and machinery of phantasmagoria. Projected
apparitions, darkness and the repurposed former religious setting made ghosts
into a paid technological spectacle. The horror is not a claim that a ghost was
real; the subject is how convincingly fear can be produced. The monument itself
still shows the terrified audience, funerary imagery and balloon/scientific
career, so this is not a story attached to an empty address.

### 2. Kardec — communication is believed and practiced

At Kardec the route changes category. The dolmen-like monument, bust,
inscription and documented petition/thanksgiving practice turn the dead from an
image into a claimed participant. The route neither mocks nor endorses
Spiritism. It asks what emotional and intellectual need is met by a system in
which death does not end communication. If flowers, notes or visitors are
present, they are observed respectfully, never promised or treated as theatre.

### 3. Victor Noir — a real killing becomes a later bodily ritual

Noir supplies the real-crime anchor. Pierre Bonaparte killed the journalist in
1870; Dalou’s later recumbent bronze made the political death public. A much
later fertility/luck custom changed how visitors used and physically altered
the monument’s surface. The route ends not by asserting a haunting, but by
showing a real murdered body transformed into a site where the living ask for
life, luck or fertility.

The narrative therefore moves through three different relationships to the
dead:

> **staged apparition → claimed communication → real violent death transformed
> by public ritual**

That arc is stronger than a generic “haunted Paris” checklist and stronger than
collecting crime addresses where nothing can be seen.

## Why it is compact instead of seven kilometres

The strongest objects are all inside Père-Lachaise. Their honest meaningful
movement is approximately 2.5–4 km. A seven-kilometre label would require one of
the following bad choices:

- cemetery wandering among unrelated celebrity graves;
- a long approach from Tour Saint-Jacques/Nerval whose street kilometres add
  little paranormal evidence;
- a cross-city detour to the unmarked Montparnasse “Vampire” crime area;
- Picpus, whose burial/martyr memory is powerful but did not pass the paranormal
  screen;
- Garnier/Phantom or cinema-monster material already owned by other routes and
  later media periods.

The project’s own design rule says not to manufacture a numerical quota. The
correct response is to keep the highlight compact, not to weaken it until it
looks like a full walking day on paper.

## Relationship to other dark routes

The portfolio contains a **dark thematic family**, not three copies of the same
walk:

- **The Dead Become Public** owns phantasmagoria, Spiritism, funerary ritual and
  the transformation of a real murder victim’s monument.
- **Cinema, Projection, and Modern Monsters** owns screen memory, Belphégor,
  Éléonore and modern mediated monsters.
- **City Under the City** owns quarry geology, collapse danger, extraction,
  ossuary reuse and underground control; it is conditional because its surface
  spine remains short.
- **Medieval Devils and Miracles** owns premodern belief, sacred efficacy,
  demonic fiction boundaries and miracle culture.

They may share mood, but they ask different questions. None replaces the
paranormal signature experience.

## Scheduling priority

When the dated itinerary is built:

1. reserve a daylight Père-Lachaise block for this route before assigning all
   green/landscape walks;
2. allow 2–3 hours rather than treating 2.5–4 km as a disposable one-hour stop;
3. avoid pairing it with another cemetery, memorial or emotionally heavy route;
4. recheck cemetery gates, Toussaint arrangements and division access;
5. keep voices low and yield to mourners, ceremonies and individual devotion;
6. do not touch monuments or imitate the Victor Noir custom;
7. if weather or access cancels it, reschedule rather than replace it with a
   generic ghost tour.

It can share a day with rest, food, friend time or a physically lighter activity.
It does not need a second unrelated module merely to “complete” a day.

## Portfolio correction

The hierarchy is now:

- **15 viable full-walk architectures**, from which a realistic trip selects
  roughly 10–12 plus situational choices;
- **1 protected compact dark signature:** The Dead Become Public;
- other modules selected according to weather, museum plans and energy.

If itinerary pressure requires choosing between this compact highlight and a
provisional seven-to-eight-kilometre landscape walk, protect the paranormal
highlight. Distance is a route-classification tool, not a measure of importance.

## Bottom line

The paranormal/horror/real-crime route survived in its strongest honest form.
What failed was not the theme; what failed was the attempt to make three
Père-Lachaise chapters look like a seven-kilometre walking day. The previous v6
summary mistakenly allowed “module” to sound like “secondary.” This correction
makes the intended decision explicit: **compact, essential, and one of the
project’s signature experiences.**
