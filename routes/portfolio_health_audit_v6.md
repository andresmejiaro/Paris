# Portfolio health audit — overlap, coverage and narrative strength

Controller audit, 3 October 2026. Read with `portfolio_rebuild_v6.md`. This
audit answers a different question from the geometry gate: not “can fifteen
spines reach seven kilometres?”, but “does each walk earn its time, and does
the set produce a varied Paris rather than fifteen differently titled passes
through the same postcard?”

## Method and confidence

Three dimensions are scored separately.

1. **Route health** combines geometry, proportion of meaningful-to-connector
   walking, reversals, access dependencies and whether a weak stop is carrying
   the distance pass.
2. **Narrative strength** tests whether the stops make a developing argument,
   whether the final chapter answers the opening, and whether the physical
   object can support the claim. It does not reward a route merely for having
   many researched facts.
3. **Portfolio value** measures geographic and experiential distinctness.

The overlap lengths below are **planning bands**, not GIS intersection results.
The repository preserves waypoint routes and leg totals, but not a common set
of street polylines for all fifteen walks. A band therefore means likely shared
public realm between named anchors; it must not be reported as measured shared
pavement. “Same district” is recorded separately from “same corridor.”

Status vocabulary:

- **GREEN:** healthy architecture and strong narrative identity.
- **GREEN/AMBER:** keep, with one named production condition.
- **AMBER:** plausible full walk, but a weak chapter, long connector, access
  dependency or incomplete narration still threatens the experience.
- **PROVISIONAL:** geometry and theme pass, but the route has not received the
  waypoint-level narrative work needed to call it visitor-ready.

## Executive diagnosis

The portfolio is **healthy as an architecture, not yet as fifteen finished
walks**. Its strongest feature is thematic range: intimacy, revolution,
occupation, espionage, performance, migration/exchange, water, canals and four
different landscape-infrastructure arguments. Its main weakness is uneven
development. Eleven routes inherit substantial narration research; the four
new landscape walks—Northwestern, Elevated East, Southern Margins and Western
Green Machinery—currently have compelling theses and measured spines but not
equivalent stop-by-stop scripts.

The central postcard zone is busy, but long exact-corridor duplication is low.
The real repetition risk is **landmark reuse** at Bastille, the Louvre/Palais-
Royal area, and the western Seine. This is manageable only if ownership stays
strict. The green routes greatly improve 12th–17th-arrondissement coverage and
prevent the portfolio from becoming an inner-six-arrondissements history set.

Recommended portfolio tiers:

- **Lead full walks:** Revolutionary, Occupied, Lovers, Water and Canal.
- **Protect as a compact signature:** The Dead Become Public; its priority does
  not depend on full-walk classification.
- **Keep with a named condition:** Cinema, Republic, Spy, Art Nouveau, Stage,
  Cosmopolitan.
- **Do not advertise as finished until scripts exist:** Northwestern,
  Elevated East, Southern Margins, Western Green Machinery.
- **Protect regardless of its shorter distance class:** The Dead Become Public,
  the Robertson→Kardec→Victor Noir paranormal/horror/real-crime signature
  experience.
- **Add to correct the period gap:** From Crime Story to Case File, a compact
  twentieth-century/contemporary true-crime module about archives, forensic
  evidence, policing and institutional myth, with Place Diana as an optional
  fact/dispute/myth satellite rather than a sanitized memorial aside.
- **Investigate rather than suppress:** Violette Nozière at rue de Madagascar
  and the 6.747 km Landru-arrest→rue-Ordener branch. Private or replaced fabric
  may still carry mystery when exact ground and documented absence matter.
- **Apply one dark-route standard:** verify the human event, attribute witness
  claims, establish that folklore circulated, preserve unresolved questions and
  retain necessary hard detail. See `mystery_narration_protocol_v6.md`.
- **Reaudit layered sites:** do not require a plaque, grave or visible paranormal
  mechanism. Gates, dark windows, exact ground, replacement fabric and absence
  can be strong encounters when documented history and traceable legend converge.
  See `layered_dark_waypoint_reassessment_v6.md`.

No pair currently appears to repeat **more than roughly 1.1 km of the same

## Geographic coverage

### Strongly covered

- **Historic centre (1st, 4th, 5th, 6th):** Revolutionary, Republic, Lovers,
  Cinema and Occupied. This is the densest cluster and needs no additional walk.
- **Western Paris (7th, 8th, 15th, 16th):** Spy, Lovers, Art Nouveau, Water and
  Western Green Machinery. Coverage is ample; future additions here should be
  rejected unless they replace a weaker route.
- **North and northeast (9th, 10th, 18th, 19th, 20th):** Stage, Cosmopolitan and
  Canal provide performance, exchange and infrastructure rather than a single
  Montmartre tourism lens.
- **South/southeast (12th, 13th, 14th):** Elevated East, Southern Margins and
  Occupied now create a meaningful counterweight to the centre.
- **17th/outer northwest:** Northwestern Belt adds the most important geographic
  expansion in v6 and reaches a metropolitan threshold rather than ending at a
  familiar inner park.

### Thin or intentionally absent

- **3rd arrondissement:** crossed by central routes but rarely the owner of a
  major chapter. This is acceptable; do not invent a Marais walk for coverage.
- **11th and 20th beyond their endpoints:** Japy, Nation and Belleville give
  exact eastern anchors, but the far eastern edge remains light. Père-Lachaise
  is deliberately a cemetery module rather than padding.
- **Southwest residential 15th:** Western Green supplies infrastructure and
  parks, not an everyday-neighbourhood route. That is sufficient unless the
  narrative commissioning fails.
- **Bois interiors:** both Bois are represented through transformation and
  management, not recreational loops. This is a feature, not a deficit.

The portfolio should **not chase arrondissement completeness**. It already
corrects the earlier central-west bias while preserving strong themes.

## Portfolio-level narrative balance

The fifteen full walks are not all versions of political history:

- **Power, citizenship and memory:** Revolutionary, Republic, Occupied, Spy.
- **Intimacy and representation:** Lovers.
- **Arts and performed images:** Cinema, Art Nouveau, Stage.
- **Exchange and translation:** Cosmopolitan.
- **Urban systems and transformed infrastructure:** Water, Canal.
- **Constructed nature and metropolitan margins:** Northwestern, Elevated,
  Southern, Western.

**The Dead Become Public** is the compact dark signature: phantasmagoria,
Spiritism and the later ritual life of a real murder victim’s monument. The v7
central extension remains a separate narration workbench, not approach mileage.
**From Crime Story to Case File** corrects the modern-period gap through BiLiPo,
the Police Museum and former “36,” with an optional transit coda to the current
Bastion/Tribunal complex.

This balance is good, but the final group is currently over-represented in
architecture and under-represented in finished voice. The four green routes
must not receive interchangeable scripts about “nature reclaiming industry.”
Their distinct narrative verbs should be:

- **Northwestern:** defend, sever, reconnect, construct;
- **Elevated:** reuse, manufacture, manage, cultivate;
- **Southern:** bury, constrain, adapt, plan;
- **Western:** abandon, preserve, connect, domesticate.

If commissioning cannot sustain those differences, keep Southern and Elevated
first, then choose between Northwestern and Western rather than publishing four
similar green walks.

## Hard decisions before visitor-ready status

1. **Do not equate geometry acceptance with route completion.** The pool has
   fifteen viable architectures, not fifteen equally mature products.
2. **Test Cinema’s final 1.38 km.** La Fayette must provide narrative payoff,
   not merely the kilometre that turns 5.586 into 6.968 km.
3. **Protect honesty on Republic.** The longer Marais/Cité crossing is a chosen
   transition and should be pleasant and clear, but must not be disguised as a
   fourth or fifth evidence chapter.
4. **Keep Spy conditional.** Passy plus Beauharnais carry it; Mata Hari and the
   Arc cannot replace those anchors.
5. **Write the four landscape routes independently.** Each needs visible object
   prompts, a midpoint turn in the argument, and an ending that resolves rather
   than merely stops.
6. **Create common route polylines before scheduling.** Intersect them to replace
   the planning overlap bands above with measured shared pavement, then avoid
   placing routes with the same landmark cluster on adjacent days.
7. **Retain the modules.** Medieval Devils, Cemetery Spirits, Markets, Books and
   the colonial/science layers supply famous-place and subject coverage without
   being inflated into full walks.

## Bottom line

Keep all fifteen at the **architecture gate**. Five are strong enough to lead
the portfolio, six are healthy only with explicit conditions, and four are
promising but narratively provisional. Paris coverage is broad and no major
tourist landmark omission justifies another route. The next quality gain comes
from stronger endings, distinct scripts for the four landscape walks, and
measured overlap polylines—not from discovering more stops.
