# Paris Walking Portfolio Rebuild v3 — Ruthless Architecture Pass

Working controller architecture, 3 October 2026. This pass tests theme and walk shape only. It is not detailed evidence QA, mapped routing, current-access verification, or external evaluation.

## Result

Recommend **18 full-route commissions**, not twenty. Of these:

- **13 are strongly thematic historical/cultural walks**;
- **4 are landscape-led walks** where rail, water, park, or promenade transformation is genuinely the subject;
- **1 cemetery/spirit route requires anchor discovery before it is safely commissionable**.

The old twenty-file portfolio is not preserved. Medieval Devils, Colonial Afterlives, Paris Reads, Scientific Paris, and Paris of Illusions survive only as modules or separate non-walking-day material. No route should be stretched solely to reach 7 km.

Decision terms:

- **KEEP:** architecture is coherent enough for route commissioning after real mapping.
- **REBUILD:** theme deserves a route, but spine or anchors must change before waypoint-level work.
- **KILL:** not a full walking route; preserve only useful modules.

## Paranormal boundary test

The four v2 paranormal-adjacent routes were too many.

1. **Medieval Devils and Miracles:** distinct period and epistemic register, but only three strong exterior chapters and roughly 4–5 meaningful kilometres. It becomes a reserve module rather than padded full route.
2. **Spirits and Cemetery Ritual:** distinct because its subject is public relationships with the dead—spectacle, doctrine, petition, fertility—not underground horror. It can survive only if Père-Lachaise supplies one additional non-duplicative anchor or an internal walking design that is interesting in itself.
3. **City Under the City:** distinct and strong. Its subject is geology, lawful thresholds, quarry instability, and the invention of an underworld.
4. **Modern Monsters:** too thin alone and overlapped cinema. It merges with Cinema into a media/projection/fear route.

The final architecture therefore has **two secure paranormal routes, one conditional spirit route, and one compact medieval module**.

## Full-route commissions

### 1. The Dead Become Public — Spirits, Spectacle, and Cemetery Ritual

**Decision:** REBUILD / conditional commission.

**Thesis:** Nineteenth-century Paris gave the dead new public roles as projected entertainment, organized doctrine, political symbol, and recipient of continuing requests.

**Anchor:** Père-Lachaise: Robertson → Allan Kardec → Victor Noir.

**Coherent spine:** begin at the cemetery’s main gate; use Robertson, Kardec, and Victor Noir as the three established chapters; add one further cemetery or immediate-neighbourhood anchor only if it contributes a genuinely different relationship with the dead. Finish at Gambetta or the eastern cemetery edge. Nerval/Tour Saint-Jacques is removed from the default because the cross-city approach is walking mileage without enough thematic density.

**Rough distance confidence:** **low-medium**. The cemetery can plausibly yield 5–7 km through actual paths and elevation, but the current three-stop experience may still be a 2–3.5 km module. Never route artificial loops among famous graves.

**Absorbed modules:** Robertson, Kardec, Victor Noir; optional compact Nerval prelude remains separate.

**Unresolved anchor gap:** one additional strong and physically valuable chapter inside or directly beside Père-Lachaise, or a mapped internal route whose landscape/memorial form itself earns the missing distance. If neither exists, demote the whole proposal to a compact cemetery module.

### 2. The City Under the City — Quarries, Horror, and Invented Underworlds

**Decision:** KEEP.

**Thesis:** Real geology, extraction, collapse risk, and managed underground thresholds repeatedly become material for crime panics, monsters, and labyrinth stories.

**Anchor:** Denfert Catacombs threshold and Montsouris quarry relief.

**Coherent spine:** Montparnasse Cemetery “vampire” context → Denfert/Guillaumot/Catacombs threshold → René-Coty exit context → lawful rail/quarry landscape transition → Parc Montsouris quarry relief → Butte-aux-Cailles high ground.

**Rough distance confidence:** **medium-high, 7–9 km**. The southward/eastward line is geographically compact and the changing ground remains relevant throughout.

**Absorbed modules:** old Paris Below surface material; Montparnasse “vampire” reserve; Catacombs film context only where it clarifies the invented underworld.

**Unresolved anchor gaps:** exact lawful connector between René-Coty, Montsouris, and Butte-aux-Cailles; avoid duplicating Route 17’s broader southern-landscape narration.

### 3. Paris Learns to Fear Its Own Image — Cinema, Projection, and Modern Monsters

**Decision:** REBUILD by merging v2 Routes 4 and 17.

**Thesis:** Projection technologies and screen stories teach audiences to imagine a second Paris behind museum walls, beneath streets, and inside familiar monuments.

**Anchor:** active cinema culture at Pathé/Champo plus Louvre/Belphégor.

**Coherent spine:** Fondation Pathé exterior → Saint-Étienne-du-Mont screen-memory connector → Champo/Champollion repertory cluster → Seine crossing → Louvre courts/Belphégor → rue du Pont-Neuf/Éléonore → Palais Garnier/Phantom **or** Grand Rex/modern projection-house finale → 145 rue La Fayette only if the chosen finale makes it a natural endpoint.

**Rough distance confidence:** **medium, 8–10 km**, but only after choosing one northern ending. Garnier and Grand Rex plus La Fayette should not all be retained if they force a hook-shaped walk.

**Absorbed modules:** Modern Monsters, Belphégor→Éléonore, strongest street-facing Cinema material, optional active screening.

**Killed material:** Catacombs duplication, ghost stations, Trocadéro clandestine cinema, generic filming-location trivia.

**Unresolved anchor gap:** decide **Garnier/Phantom versus Grand Rex/projection culture** based on thematic and physical strength; test whether 145 La Fayette is a payoff or merely an extra kilometre.

### 4. When the Street Took Power — Revolutionary Paris

**Decision:** KEEP.

**Thesis:** Commercial and ceremonial spaces became stages where crowds challenged sovereignty, experienced victory, and confronted punishment or restoration.

**Anchor:** Palais-Royal → Concorde/Tuileries → Hôtel de Ville → Bastille.

**Coherent spine:** Palais-Royal café de Foy → Concorde → Tuileries/Carrousel → Hôtel de Ville → Bastille → optional Henri-Galli masonry.

**Rough distance confidence:** **high, 8–9 km**. The westward opening and return east are narratively deliberate, though external mapping must ensure they do not feel like needless backtracking.

**Unresolved anchor gap:** none architectural; test whether Concorde-before-Tuileries chronology remains clear in live delivery.

### 5. Who Gets to Be France? — Republic and Representation

**Thesis:** The Republic makes promises in public sculpture, selects exemplary citizens, and conducts representative government inside inherited monumental forms.

**Anchor:** République, Panthéon exterior, Palais Bourbon.

**Coherent spine:** République monument → central civic streets → Panthéon exterior → Luxembourg/Sénat exterior → Palais Bourbon/Pont de la Concorde.

**Absorbed modules:** Luxembourg pause; Nation/Dalou becomes a reserve eastern Republic module.

**Unresolved anchor gap:** one meaningful transition between République and Panthéon that is not Hôtel de Ville duplication or a generic landmark stop.

### 6. Occupied City — Constraint, Resistance, Return, Absence

**Decision:** REBUILD geometry; keep theme strongly.

**Thesis:** Occupation transformed ordinary movement, documents, buildings, and addresses; resistance and return never erase persecution and absence.

**Anchor:** Lutetia → 48 rue du Four → Shoah memorial complex.

**Coherent spine:** Denfert wartime command-site exterior/context → boulevard Raspail north to Lutetia → 48 rue du Four → east through Saint-Germain and the Seine crossing → Shoah Memorial/Mur des Justes exterior complex.

**Rough distance confidence:** **medium-high, roughly 6–8 km before local observation and memorial circulation**. This is already a plausible real walk; do not force a western Vel d’Hiv detour.

**Absorbed modules:** exterior daily-life/identity-control material from the old museum opening; Lutetia; CNR address; Shoah ending.

**Killed material:** Vel d’Hiv as a route detour, Hôtel de Ville liberation finale, Invalides chronology, random plaque collecting.

**Unresolved anchor gap:** Denfert exterior must sustain a genuine opening without museum objects. If it cannot, begin farther north with one exact everyday-occupation anchor rather than lengthening westward.

### 7. Who Reads Your Papers? — Spy Paris

**Decision:** REBUILD / protect if viable.

**Thesis:** Espionage works through privileged access, copied documents, trusted intermediaries, institutional certainty, and legends that obscure operational reality.

**Anchor:** Hôtel Beauharnais and the Dreyfus evidence chain.

**Coherent spine:** Franklin/Bancroft Passy footprint → Trocadéro diplomatic landscape → École Militaire/Dreyfus public consequence → Hôtel Beauharnais → Mata Hari former hotel block → Champs/Arc de Triomphe public-image coda.

**Rough distance confidence:** **medium, 9–11 km**. The route moves east from Passy, then north across the Seine to the Champs; the shape is plausible, but Beauharnais→Champs must be mapped carefully.

**Absorbed modules:** Dreyfus connective; famous western monuments only as state/display context.

**Unresolved anchor gaps:** present physical legibility at Passy and Mata Hari’s construction-obscured block. If both fail, redesign with new exact operations; do not retain an archival nine-kilometre walk around one good façade. Spy remains a priority commission, not an automatic acceptance.

### 8. Who Tells the Love Story? — Public Intimacy and Parisian Myth

### 13. The Metabolism of a City — Water, Pressure, Flood, and Waste

**Decision:** KEEP with a surface-only rebuild.

**Anchor:** Square Lamartine artesian water and Pont de l’Alma/Zouave.

**Coherent spine:** Square Lamartine → Passy reservoir exterior → Auteuil pumping works exterior → selected Wallace fountain → Seine infrastructure → Pont de l’Alma/Zouave → one truthful sewer-system surface endpoint.

**Rough distance confidence:** **medium, 8–10 km**. Western anchors form a credible system line; the final sewer chapter must not require a long eastward detour.

**Absorbed modules:** old Engineered City exterior material.

**Unresolved anchor gap:** a physically legible surface waste/sewer endpoint near the Seine finish. If none exists, finish at flood/water management and treat waste as a separate museum-day subject.

- **Route 13 Metabolism** owns potable water/flood/waste; **Route 14 Canal** owns navigation and land-use change.
