# Paris Portfolio Rebuild v4 — Geometry Gate Decisions

Controller delta, 3 October 2026. This document records only changes from v3 and the resulting definitive candidate list. Distances are honest planning bands based on Paris street geography and expected local circulation, not GPS tracks. No historical evidence audit or external evaluation has occurred.

## Gate results

### Route 1 — Spirits and Cemetery Ritual

**Decision: DEMOTE TO MODULE.**

Robertson (division 8), Kardec (division 44), and Victor Noir (division 92) create a satisfying internal progression, but their meaningful cemetery path is not plausibly a 7–11 km thematic walk. Père-Lachaise is large enough to manufacture distance by wandering; that is not the same as having chapters. Nerval/Tour Saint-Jacques would add a cross-city transfer or a long low-density approach.

Definitive form: **2.5–4 km / 1.5–2.5 hours**, main entrance → Robertson → Kardec → Victor Noir → Gambetta-side exit. Preserve as a strong morning/afternoon module. A fourth grave is not commissioned merely to rescue route status.

Portfolio consequence: the full-route count drops by one.

### Route 3 — Cinema, Projection, and Modern Monsters

**Decision: KEEP AFTER MERGE.**

Definitive northbound spine:

**Fondation Pathé exterior → Saint-Étienne-du-Mont screen-memory connector → Champo/Champollion → rue du Pont-Neuf/Éléonore → Louvre courts/Belphégor → Grand Rex → 145 rue La Fayette.**

This ordering avoids the old Louvre→Garnier westward pull followed by a northeast return. Grand Rex lies on the natural northward progression from the Louvre toward La Fayette and supplies actual projection-house scale. Garnier leaves this route entirely.

**Honest band: 7–9 km. Confidence: medium-high.** Pathé to the Latin Quarter is the longest opening movement but remains consistent with cinema institutions and street image. Grand Rex and La Fayette require cheap site-value confirmation; if La Fayette feels like an engineering façade bolted onto the ending, finish at Grand Rex and accept a roughly 6–7 km compact route.

Ownership: Route 3 owns Belphégor, Éléonore, Grand Rex, and 145 La Fayette. It does not own Garnier/Phantom or the Catacombs.

### Route 5 — Republic and Representation

**Decision: KEEP; NO ARTIFICIAL TRANSITION STOP.**

Definitive spine:

**République → direct Marais/Cité street transition → Panthéon exterior → Luxembourg/Sénat exterior → Palais Bourbon/Pont de la Concorde.**

République to Panthéon is a real central crossing of roughly 3–4 km. It does not need a fifth narrated institution merely because the route spends time walking. Hôtel de Ville would duplicate Revolutionary Paris; Place des Vosges or Notre-Dame would be checklist stops. The transition should be treated as ordinary city texture and rest/food opportunity.

**Honest band: 7–8.5 km. Confidence: high.** Nation remains a reserve module, not the opening.

Unresolved gap: none. The challenge is delivery rhythm, not missing geography.

### Route 7 — Spy Paris

**Decision: CONDITIONAL REBUILD; SHAPE PASSES, ENDPOINTS DO NOT YET PASS.**

Provisional spine:

**Passy/Valentinois marker area → Trocadéro diplomatic landscape → École Militaire → Hôtel Beauharnais → Seine crossing/Champs approach → Mata Hari former hotel block → Arc de Triomphe coda.**

The overall shape is viable: east from Passy to École Militaire and Beauharnais, then north/northwest to the Champs. The Arc is not a spy site; it is a short public-image coda contrasting hidden evidence with national display.

**Honest band: 9–11 km. Confidence: medium for geometry, low for endpoint experience.**

Preliminary legibility decision:

- Passy remains weak until the exact marker/public footprint is confirmed. A vanished headquarters plus archival story cannot carry the opening unaided.
- Mata Hari remains weak while redevelopment obscures the arrest block. A street number behind construction is not a payoff.
- Beauharnais remains the reliable anchor; École Militaire remains a useful consequence stop.

Commission condition: at least one endpoint must pass current visual validation and a second exact operation must support Beauharnais. If both Passy and Mata Hari fail, do not walk this shape. Return Spy for replacement-anchor discovery while preserving the theme as a priority.

### Route 10 — Paris on Stage After Dark

**Decision: KEEP WITH REORDERING; GARNIER ASSIGNED HERE.**

Definitive spine:

**Folies Bergère → Palais Garnier → Casino de Paris → Moulin Rouge → Lapin Agile/Sacré-Cœur hill chapter → Madame Arthur downhill finale.**

Folies first removes the former eastward detour. Garnier establishes institutional spectacle; Casino and Moulin shift into commercial production; Lapin/Sacré-Cœur examine bohemian and tourist staging; Madame Arthur provides the living contemporary ending. If exact opening hours make Madame Arthur-first activity preferable, the final Montmartre loop may reverse, but Garnier stays between Folies and the Clichy/Pigalle corridor.

**Honest band: 6.5–8 km. Confidence: medium-high.** This is accepted slightly below the ordinary target because the climb, evening observation, and substantial venue stories create a real half-day experience. Do not add a remote venue to reach a number.

Ownership: Garnier/Phantom and theatrical machinery belong to Route 10. Route 3 uses Grand Rex instead.

Unresolved gap: Lapin Agile must contribute a distinct chanson/work/audience chapter; otherwise omit it and accept the more compact route.

### Route 11 — Cosmopolitan North and East

**Decision: KEEP WITH WEST-TO-EAST ORDER.**

Definitive spine:

**Passage Brady → Bouffes du Nord → Goutte-d’Or/ICI → Belleville crossroads/rue Dénoyez → Parc de Belleville finale.**

This eliminates the old Belleville→Brady→Goutte-d’Or hook. The route moves north from Brady to Bouffes/ICI, then east to Belleville and climbs to a viewpoint that lets the route end with the scale of the translated city rather than another commercial passage.

**Honest band: 7.5–9.5 km. Confidence: medium.** The ICI→Belleville leg is the longest connector; it must be mapped on active streets and may warrant transit only if the resulting walk loses thematic density. If so, Passage Brady is the first candidate to remove, not Belleville or ICI.

Unresolved gap: confirm whether the north-to-east connector offers enough urban continuity without classifying residents as evidence. No extra “ethnic quarter” stops should be invented.

### Route 13 — Water, Pressure, and Flood

**Decision: REBUILD AND NARROW.**

New title: **The Metabolism of a City — Water, Pressure, and Flood.** Waste is removed from the surface route because no credible nearby exterior endpoint equals the former sewer interior.

Definitive spine:

**Square Lamartine artesian fountain → selected public-water comparison near the western cluster → Auteuil pumping works exterior → Pont Mirabeau/Seine infrastructure → eastward river walk → Pont de l’Alma/Zouave → sewer-museum entrance only as an optional infrastructure handoff, not a narrated core.**

Passy reservoir is removed if it creates a north/east detour before Auteuil. The route should move south toward Auteuil, then east with the Seine. The Zouave is the proper physical payoff: water that the city tries to control becomes a public measure of threat.

**Honest band: 8–10 km. Confidence: medium.** Exact fountain/comparison selection controls whether the opening zigzags.

Absorbed/killed: potable-water and flood material retained; waste/sewer history moves to museum-day material. Éléonore remains with Route 3.

Unresolved gap: select one western comparison that lies naturally between Lamartine and Auteuil. Do not preserve both reservoir and Wallace fountain if they pull in different directions.

### Route 15 — Northwestern Belt

**Decision: KEEP; PORTE MAILLOT WINS.**

Definitive spine:

**Batignolles village streets → Square des Batignolles → Parc Clichy-Batignolles–Martin-Luther-King → accessible Petite Ceinture 17th → Promenade Pereire → Porte Maillot/outer-edge transformation.**

Porte Maillot continues the westward movement and completes the thesis with infrastructure and metropolitan edge. Parc Monceau would require a southeast return and change the payoff into another curated park after two parks already appeared.

**Honest band: 8–10 km. Confidence: medium-high**, subject to current Petite Ceinture entrance/exit continuity.

Unresolved gap: the Porte Maillot ending needs one exact, publicly legible transformation chapter rather than a generic traffic interchange.

## Cross-route ownership decisions

### Palais Garnier

**Owner: Paris on Stage After Dark.**

Its primary role is theatre as urban spectacle, labor, audience, and cultural afterlife. Cinema, Projection, and Modern Monsters takes Grand Rex for projection and modern media fear. Belphégor remains that route’s fictional-monument anchor.

### Bir-Hakeim

**Narrative owner: Lovers’ Paris.**

Lovers’ Paris uses couples, photography, and the manufacture of a Paris-romance image. The western landscape route must not end at Bir-Hakeim with a second substantial narration.

Revised Route 18 endpoint: **Île aux Cygnes/Pont de Grenelle river-engineering landscape**, or another pre-Bir-Hakeim endpoint that preserves the rail/park/river thesis. Passing views of Bir-Hakeim are allowed; revisiting it as a core is not.

### Parc Montsouris / quarry relief

**Quarry-relief owner: City Under the City.**

Southern Margins may traverse Parc Montsouris as planted/rail-edge landscape but omits the quarry-relief narration. If its most coherent line does not need the park, it may finish at Cité Universitaire instead.
