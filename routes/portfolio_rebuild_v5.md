Gate rules used here:

- **FULL WALK PASS:** at least 7 km actual routed thematic spine, or an explicitly justified substantial-experience exception.
- **REBUILD:** viable theme requiring one meaningful anchor/corridor decision; the gap is named below.
- **MODULE/FAIL:** valuable experience but not a 7–11 km walking day. No padding commission.
