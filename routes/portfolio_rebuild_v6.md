## Decision

The portfolio now contains **15 viable full-walk architectures**, including two
deliberate substantial-experience exceptions and several routes with explicit
operational conditions. They are not fifteen equally finished route products:
the four new landscape routes remain narratively provisional. That is enough
architecture. Do not seek an eighteenth or twentieth
route by padding. One strong theme, City Under the City, remains just below the
measured threshold and is retained as a conditional candidate/module.

Fifteen is an **architecture pool**, not a two-week completion quota.
The likely trip outcome is 10–12 high-confidence full walks plus two or three
choices governed by weather, programmes, access and energy. New discovery may replace a failed architecture; it must not manufacture an
additional route merely because an interesting unused waypoint exists. The
rejected v7 cross-city paranormal extension is the controlling example: its
3.995 km chapterless transfer does not become thematic mileage merely because
the total clears a threshold. See `cruce_context_review_v8.md`.

The v5 rules remain controlling:

- a normal full walk needs at least 7 km of routed thematic spine;
- an exception needs a substantial experience, not invented circulation;
- paid interiors, closed paths, park laps, lodging approaches and exit travel
  contribute zero;
- modules remain useful trip material and do not need promotion.

## Discovery decisions now frozen

### Revolutionary and Occupied

Nation/Dalou wins over Maison Belhomme and the Mur des Fédérés. It is a natural
2.095 km continuation beyond Bastille and supplies a public argument about the
state's later absorption of revolutionary imagery. The Mur option is rejected
because it is gate-dependent, collides with the cemetery module, and would make
cemetery navigation carry the route.

Gymnase Japy wins for Occupied City because the ordinary municipal building and
its exterior memorial make the machinery of the *billet vert* roundup visible
on the natural eastbound line. It solves both the thematic and geometry gaps
without Vel d'Hiv, a generic plaque collection, or an interior.

### Spy, Cosmopolitan and the northwestern edge

Spy's distance was never the problem. The corrected Passy stop at the
Singer/Raynouard inscription, together with Beauharnais, clears the physical-
anchor gate. Recheck the inscription and Champs hoarding close to travel. If
Passy is unexpectedly inaccessible, demote/rebuild rather than asking the Mata
Hari worksite to carry the route.

CENTQUATRE earns its place through production, residency and institutional
exchange, not through the origin of nearby residents. Its insertion adds 1.473
km net and brings Cosmopolitan to a real pass. The strongest November example
is programme-dependent; the route must never promise an artist encounter.

The Northwestern extension is a one-way threshold traverse. Parodi explains a
fortified, severed and reconnected edge; Mare Saint-James demonstrates
constructed nature; Porte de Madrid supplies an exact designed endpoint. The
Mare deviation adds only 0.211 km over the direct Parodi→Madrid movement, so the
pass does not depend on a scenic circuit.

### Three green/southern routes

Elevated East now moves from converted railway to Alphand's constructed wood
and finally to contemporary municipal agroecological management at the Ferme.
Do not substitute the Arboretum while its official storm closure remains.

Southern Margins no longer depends on rail. Its thesis is difficult ground and
urban response: buried river, light housing on former wet ground, planted
quarry relief, and planned campus edge. This is both stronger and safer than
implying access to closed PC13.

Western Green Machinery now has two materially different retired-rail
conversions. PC14 supplies the relatively wild cutting; lawful streets bridge
to Brassens; the City's August 2026 PC15 connection supports the continuous
Dantzig→Balard chapter. Never imply that PC14 and PC15 connect by rail.

## Conditional candidate — do not count yet

### City Under the City

The Grenelle-borehole → cemetery → Denfert/Guillaumot → Catacombs-exit →
Montsouris/La Carrière → Paul-Verlaine line is thematically promising as a walk
about boring, extraction, collapse and control. The reproduced spine is only
**6.793 km**, however. It becomes roughly 7–7.5 km only if one already-approved
cemetery chapter produces honest mapped circulation.

Therefore it is **not walk 16 yet**. Select and validate that chapter before
counting it, keep quarry/fontis danger central, and prevent the two deep wells
from turning this into a duplicate municipal-water route. If no chapter earns
the circulation, retain the material as a strong 5–7 km module.
