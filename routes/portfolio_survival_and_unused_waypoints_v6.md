# Portfolio survival and unused-waypoint register

> **Context-review correction:** `cruce_context_review_v8.md` rejects the v7
> cross-city extension as manufactured mileage. Fifteen full-walk architectures
> and the protected compact paranormal signature now control.

Controller clarification, 3 October 2026. This file answers three practical
questions left open by the v6 geometry and health documents:

1. Is fifteen the right number of full-walk architectures?
2. What survived from the original portfolio?
3. Which researched or proposed waypoints are deliberately in no current route?

“In no route” below means no accepted v6 full walk or retained module uses the
place as a baseline narrated waypoint. A route may pass it incidentally. Old
route files preserve research history and sometimes describe an option that a
later controller decision rejected; v6 controls.

## Controller opinion: fifteen is a pool, not a quota

**Do not add another full walk.** Fifteen is already the upper healthy bound for
this trip and for the current evidence. It is not a recommendation to complete
all fifteen. A realistic trip portfolio is:

- **five full-walk flagships** that deserve priority;
- **six conditional full-walk architectures** selected according to programme,
  access, weather and interest;
- **four narratively provisional landscape walks**, of which perhaps two to
  four will survive commissioning;
- compact modules for short days, bad weather, museum time and recovery.

One compact experience is categorically different from the general reserve
modules: **The Dead Become Public** (Robertson→Kardec→Victor Noir) is the
protected paranormal/horror/real-crime highlight. It should be scheduled ahead
of provisional landscape walks even though its honest spine is only 2.5–4 km.

A second protected compact block now corrects the period imbalance: **From Crime
Story to Case File** uses BiLiPo, the Police Museum and former 36 quai des
Orfèvres to examine twentieth-century cases, forensic evidence, police myth and
the move to twenty-first-century justice institutions. Place Diana/Pont de
l’Alma is its optional modern-mystery satellite, organized around what is fact,
what proceedings decided, what remains disputed and what later became myth. It
is deliberately not a serial-killer-address walk.

The broader bench is not closed: `true_crime_across_eras_v6.md` promotes
Violette Nozière’s rue de Madagascar site for physical audit and preserves a
6.747 km institutional→Landru→Bonnot northbound branch as a conditional full
walk. “Not yet accepted” now means evidence/visibility work remains, not that
street crime and unresolved cases are unwelcome.

The practical outcome is likely **10–12 high-confidence full walks plus two or
three situational choices**, not fifteen compulsory outings. That already fills
a two-week trip generously when friend time, rest, laundry, food and one free-
museum day are protected.

Another full route would almost certainly do one of four unhealthy things:

1. repeat the central postcard field around Louvre/Marais/Seine;
2. repeat a green-conversion story already assigned to one of four landscape
   walks;
3. promote a 2–5 km module by attaching a remote endpoint;
4. use a famous but narratively weak address to manufacture seven kilometres.

New research is justified only as a **replacement** for a failing route, not as
an additional route. The clearest replacement triggers are: La Fayette fails Cinema,
Passy fails Spy, an active-night programme fails Stage, or one of the four
landscape walks cannot sustain distinct narration.

### The five strongest full-walk survivors

If the portfolio had to shrink immediately, protect:

1. Revolutionary Paris;
2. Occupied City;
3. Lovers’ Paris;
4. Water, Pressure, and Flood;
5. Canal Changes Jobs.

The compact **The Dead Become Public** signature remains an equal itinerary
priority outside the full-walk count.

They combine the clearest narrative development, credible physical evidence,
strong endings and useful geographic spread. They are not merely the five most
famous-landmark-heavy routes.

### The six conditional survivors

Cinema, Republic, Spy, Art Nouveau, Stage and Cosmopolitan remain worth keeping,
but each has one explicit weakness. Respectively: a distance-bearing finale,
a long un-narrated transition, fragile physical anchors, a small distance
exception, dependence on live evening conditions, and a long inter-district
connector.

### The four provisional survivors

Northwestern, Elevated, Southern and Western passed theme and geometry but not
full narrative production. Calling them “survivors” means they earned the right
to be scripted; it does not mean they are ready to publish. If two converge on
the same generic reclamation story, keep **Southern first, Elevated second**, and
make Northwestern/Western compete for the remaining slot.

## Significant waypoints deliberately in no current route

This is the no-route register. It excludes places already retained as an
optional stop inside a module and excludes a landmark merely passed on another
walk. The descriptions explain why a potentially interesting place remains
unused.

**Status correction:** “unused” no longer means “narratively failed.” Under the
layered-site rule in `layered_dark_waypoint_reassessment_v6.md`, Nerval/Tour
Saint-Jacques, the former Capucines phantasmagoria ground, Cour des Miracles,
ghost-station surface traces, Trocadéro’s clandestine-cinema story, Petiot’s
replacement address and Picpus are reopened for cheap audit. Their historical
entries below remain provenance, not final rejection rulings.

### Central, medieval and paranormal

- **Mur des Je t’aime, square Jehan-Rictus:** a readable contemporary ritual of
  declared love, but its official access was unsafe/closed when researched. It
  is not needed now that Lovers has a complete measured spine.
- **Pont-Neuf / Vert-Galant romance stop:** rejected before full investigation.
  It would repeat riverside romance and possibly become a film-location detour
  between stronger relationship stories.
- **Cour des Miracles, rue de Damiette/rue des Forges:** the location of a
  vanished court can support a valuable critique of hostile stories about poor
  residents, but almost no physical payoff survives and the northern detour
  weakens Medieval Devils.
- **Tour Saint-Jacques as a medieval-devil stop:** the tower’s strongest Satan
  material comes from Nerval’s nineteenth-century fiction, not medieval history.
  Nerval remains only an optional prelude to the Spirits module; the tower is
  not a baseline waypoint anywhere.
- **“Vampire of Montparnasse” site:** the Bertrand grave-desecration story is
  documented, but no exact surviving grave, marker or capture site was verified.
  A sensational story without a visible place does not justify a cross-city
  detour.
- **Picpus as a paranormal stop:** historically important burial ground, but no
  sufficiently distinct apparition/ritual chapter earned the route.
- **Saint-Martin and Croix-Rouge ghost stations:** interesting transit history,
  but there is no lawful surface encounter that makes the abandoned platforms
  visible enough for a narrated walking stop.
- **Les Halles underground-monster/paranormal stop:** too diffuse and dependent
  on generic underground anxiety; the central location is already covered by
  Markets and Cinema with stronger physical objects.
- **Trocadéro clandestine-cinema story:** vivid reported underground-cinema
  details remain inaccessible and disputed. The esplanade cannot honestly show
  the chamber below it. Spy may cross the district for a different reason.

### Political, occupation and military alternatives

- **Square Henri-Galli Bastille stones:** authentic relocated masonry, but the
  old endpoint required a backward 0.71 km tail after Bastille. Nation/Dalou now
  supplies a much stronger eastward conclusion.
- **Saint-Merri uprising chapter:** historically relevant, but the researched
  version leaned too heavily on literary association and would add another
  uprising before Revolutionary’s stronger anchors had room to breathe.
- **Mur des Fédérés as Revolutionary’s ending:** powerful Commune memorial, but
  cemetery gates/navigation, collision with the Spirits module and an abrupt
  later-revolution shift make it the wrong endpoint for this route.
- **Hôtel de Ville as Occupied City’s liberation finale:** deliberately rejected
  because it would turn a route about occupation, persecution and return into a
  conventional triumphant ending. The place remains in Revolutionary under a
  different story.
- **Invalides military chronology:** too institutionally broad and too western
  for Occupied City’s everyday-occupation line; it would replace, not deepen,
  the route’s thesis.
- **Vel d’Hiv site:** historically essential but geographically wrong for the
  accepted eastbound Occupied line. Japy supplies a precise roundup site without
  a remote western detour. Vel d’Hiv should return only in a redesigned route,
  never as distance padding.
- **Hôtel d’Estrées spy stop:** the proposed diplomatic/espionage claim did not
  produce a sufficiently exact, publicly legible operation to beat Beauharnais.
- **Grande Arche:** potentially strong for fraternity, bicentenary diplomacy or
  business-city architecture, but it requires a separate La Défense block. It
  cannot be appended to Cosmopolitan as a remote western finale.

### Architecture, cinema and spectacle alternatives

- **Félix Potin, 140 rue de Rennes:** viable Art Nouveau commercial spectacle,
  but geographically outside the western threshold sequence and not fully
  sourced for its strongest reinforced-concrete superlative.
- **Hôtel Guimard:** a useful comparison but biographically repetitive beside
  Castel Béranger and the Guimard cluster; it is not a baseline stop.
- **La Pagode, rue de Babylone:** architecturally distinctive cinema, but no
  dated public 2026 programme or reopening evidence justified an operational
  waypoint.
- **Musée Méliès / Cinémathèque Bercy:** strong alternative cinema institution,
  but it would require a different Bercy route and duplicate substantial indoor
  time. It is an option for replacing Pathé, not an add-on.
- **Bir-Hakeim as an *Inception* stop:** the bridge already belongs to Lovers’
  image-making finale. A film-frame checklist does not justify duplicate western
  travel. Cinema can mention neither the bridge nor the film by default.
- **Filmmaker homes and an *Amélie* address trail:** associations without a
  distinct accessible mechanism or institution; they would turn Cinema into a
  scattered location checklist.
- **Passage des Panoramas as an illusion machine:** the historical panorama is
  absent. A surviving shopping passage is not evidence of the vanished optical
  attraction.
- **Paid illusion attractions:** Paradox/Musée de l’Illusion may be enjoyable,
  but no unique Paris mechanism earned limited budget or an exterior walking
  route.
- **82 rue Réaumur:** an exuberant façade, but unusual genuine architecture is
  not automatically deception. No exact illusion mechanism was established.

### Markets, books, science and infrastructure alternatives

- **Marché Bastille as a fourth market:** active on limited days but redundant
  after Aligre and Enfants Rouges; it does not rescue Markets’ distance without
  repeating the same activity.
- **Marché Saint-Quentin:** a credible covered-market alternative, held in
  reserve for schedule failure rather than added to the default morning.
- **Imprimerie nationale’s Paris site:** no sufficiently public, legible visitor
  encounter; institutional association alone cannot carry Books.
- **Musée de la BnF:** worthwhile collection, but a third library/museum chapter
  needs a distinct material argument and extra indoor time. It is not baseline.
- **Observatoire de Paris:** routine November interior access was not verified;
  an exceptional heritage-day programme does not make a closed-gate façade a
  scientific-spectacle payoff.
- **Old Pavillon de l’Eau, 77 avenue de Versailles:** no longer the assumed
  public water museum. The new 13th-arrondissement programme is occasional and
  family/reservation-led, not a default western stop.
- **Passy reservoir walls:** historically relevant, but a wall view cannot prove
  present operating capacity and repeats the western water system without a new
  physical action.
- **Square de l’Abbé-Migne “quarry monument”:** the proposed monument was not
  verified. It cannot be used to lengthen City Under the City.
- **IGC office:** a working municipal service at a different current address,
  not a public quarry attraction.
- **Port-Mahon and Capucins quarries:** exceptional-event or unverified-access
  spaces, not lawful November baseline waypoints.

### Colonial-memory alternatives

- **Colbert at the Assemblée nationale:** a genuine contested object, but the
  proposed explanatory plaque was not verified and the façade already belongs
  to Republic. A parliamentary demand for context is not proof that context is
  installed onsite.
- **Luxembourg enslavement memorial:** valid memory work, but it creates a third
  central block and overlaps Republic/Lovers geography without improving the
  two-part colonial architecture.
- **Quai Branly provenance/restitution stop:** would require object-specific,
  current-gallery research and paid museum time. It is unsafe to imply every
  African object was stolen or that restituted objects remain displayed.
- **Jardin d’Agronomie tropicale:** too remote for the current modules and lacks
  the safe-access, object-specific investigation needed to avoid using ruined
  colonial pavilions as exotic atmosphere.

## Places that look unused but are actually owned elsewhere

Several old files say “skip” because ownership moved later. They must not be
mistaken for portfolio gaps:

- **Nation/Dalou** moved from Republic reserve to Revolutionary’s finale.
- **Bir-Hakeim** moved from an old Lovers exclusion into the measured Lovers
  finale; it remains excluded from Cinema, Water narration and Western Green.
- **Palais Garnier and Lapin Agile** belong to Stage, not Cinema or the old
  narrow Burlesque draft.
- **Arsenal** is required by the full Canal route, not merely an optional branch.
- **CENTQUATRE** is now core to Cosmopolitan.
- **Gymnase Japy** is now the Occupied endpoint.
- **PC14/PC15, Ferme de Paris, and the Bièvre/Cité Florale surface line** are
  accepted architectural spines awaiting narration, not unused leads.
- **Porte Dorée/Marchand, Notre-Dame/Sainte-Chapelle and Père-Lachaise’s three
  spirits** are modules. Their lack of full-walk status does not make them
  orphaned.

## Replacement bench, not expansion bench

If an accepted architecture fails, consider replacements in this order:

1. **Cinema failure:** redesign around Musée Méliès/Bercy, or accept Cinema as a
   compact route. Do not retain La Fayette only for distance.
2. **Spy failure:** research a new exact operation on the same west/central arc.
   Do not append Grande Arche or Invalides.
3. **Stage inactive:** demote it to a compact exterior module. Do not attach a
   remote venue.
4. **Landscape convergence:** keep Southern and Elevated first; compare the
   finished Northwestern and Western scripts rather than publishing both by
   default.
5. **Unexpected reopening/access change:** an unused waypoint may be reconsidered
   only if it improves a route’s argument and replaces a weaker stop. Reopening
   by itself does not create thematic value.

## Bottom line

Fifteen is enough and may still reduce after narrative
and operational QA. More would be overkill unless replacing a failure. The
survivors already cover central monuments, western icons, north/east cultural
production, southern margins and both Bois thresholds. The unused-waypoint list
is evidence of editorial discipline: interesting facts, famous addresses and
remote sites were rejected whenever their physical payoff, geography or
narrative role did not earn the walk.
